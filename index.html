Happy Birthday to you MITHRA ❤️🎂

Innaiku un birthday… honestly, enna sollanum nu enakku romba theriyala. Aana evlo distance irundhalum, evlo things changed irundhalum, un birthday-a just normal-a kadandhu poida ennaala mudiyala. Because once, you were such an important part of my life… and honestly, you still are in a place in my heart that nobody can simply replace.

Nee vitu ponatha accept pannika mudiyala eppadi iruku theriuma oru kannu theriyathavanuku paravi ya kuduthutu konja naal la avanga kitta irunthu pidingita eppadi irukum antha maari valikuthhu ulla.

Sometimes I wish things had ended differently. Sometimes I wish I could go back and fix the things I couldn't understand back then. But life doesn't always give us another chance to rewrite what happened. So all I can do is accept the reality, even when my heart takes longer to understand it.

My mind always think about, if i stay i lose myself, if i leave i lose you, if i wait then it will never be me, if i move on it'll never be you, so....what do i do. Do i Make a left but there's nothing right or a right where there's nothing left 😣.

But more than that i may be your Ex but you are not my Ex, Not my Past! you are the girl i still love with every part of my heart, you are the one! i dreamed of spending forever with. you are the girl who taught me what love is. both beauty and pain and you are reason I cried, the reason I believed in Love. May be you are No longer mine, but in the quietest parts of my heart, you will always remain my greatest lover story 😭

I don't know what the future has written for us. Maybe someday we'll become complete strangers, maybe we'll talk again, or maybe we'll just remain a beautiful chapter in each other's lives. I don't want to force anything or expect anything from you. I just want you to know that what I felt for you was real.

Unna pathi enakku pidicha memories, namma pesina moments, siricha moments, sanda potta moments… ellamey en mind-la somewhere irukum. Some memories don't disappear just because people do.

And today, more than anything else, I genuinely want you to be happy. Nee life-la romba nalla things achieve pannanum, un dreams ellam fulfil aaganum, unakku peace-um happiness-um kidaikanum. Even if I'm not the person standing beside you when all those things happen, I'll still be happy knowing that you're doing well.

Maybe I couldn't be the person you wanted forever… but I'll always be thankful that, for some time, I got to call you mine. ❤️

So once again, Happy Birthday. 🎂❤️ Take care of yourself, smile a lot, and never forget that you are special.

Happy Birthday, once again… from someone who still cares about you more than he knows how to explain. 🫶🏻❤️
